## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of this project seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please do not report it through public issues or discussions.

### Preferred Reporting Method
Please report security vulnerabilities confidentially:
* **Via GitHub**: Use the "Report a vulnerability" button under the **Security** tab of this repository.
* **Via Email**: Alternatively, email your report to **kasuta@riseup.net**.

### What to Include
To help us triage and resolve the issue quickly, please include:
* A **detailed description** of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
* **Steps to reproduce** the issue (including any proof-of-concept scripts or code).
* The **environment details** (OS, version, configuration) where the bug was found.

### Our Commitment
* **Acknowledgement**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within **72 hours**.
* **Updates**: We will send you status updates at least once every **7 days** until the issue is resolved.
* **Disclosure**: We follow coordinated disclosure and ask that you give us **90 days** to patch the issue before making any details public.
